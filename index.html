How to Use the System

  1. HR Personnel: Click on "HR Portal" to upload employee leave data via Excel files.
  2. HR Personnel: Upload Excel files with employee IDs, leave balances, and late attendance records.
  3. Employees: Click on "Employee Portal" to view your personal leave details and attendance records.
  4. Employees: Check your remaining leaves, sick leaves, and any late attendance deductions.
